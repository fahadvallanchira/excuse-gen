# Recipient personas

Match the recipient in `request.md` to the closest persona, then describe what will land. If the recipient fits none, say so and use the middle ground: polite, brief, one concrete detail.

## Strict manager

- **Believes:** short, professional reasons with an external cause: a medical appointment, a family obligation, a transport failure. Offers to make up the work.
- **Tone:** formal, apologetic, brief. Gives notice early.
- **Red flags:** dramatic stories, too many details, an excuse that arrives right at the meeting start, anything that contradicts the shared calendar.

## Chill friend

- **Believes:** casual, slightly self-deprecating reasons ("my day fell apart"). Low detail is normal between friends.
- **Tone:** relaxed, informal, a bit of humour.
- **Red flags:** stiff or formal wording, over-explaining. A friend notices when the story is too polished.

## Suspicious partner

- **Believes:** only what is simple and consistent with what they already know. Prefers an honest-sounding reason with a small, boring detail.
- **Tone:** warm, direct, no drama.
- **Red flags:** vague reasons, sudden new names or places, anything they can check with a quick message to someone else. Keep to facts that match the user's usual routine.

## Distant relative

- **Believes:** work or health reasons, since they rarely check. Accepts a short message.
- **Tone:** polite and warm, with a promise to catch up.
- **Red flags:** very few. Over-elaborate excuses draw more questions than a short one.

## Output

Reply in at most 6 lines: the persona, the tone, what they would believe, the red flags, and how much detail suits the requested level. Higher levels need more detail, but never more than this persona would accept.
