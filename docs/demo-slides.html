Excuse Swarm

Six AI agents argue about your alibi so you don’t have to.

Welcome. This is a swarm of specialist agents that writes an excuse, then tries to break it before you ever see it. Nothing is sent automatically: you approve everything.

The problem

Most excuses fail the same way: someone checks.

Clashes with the calendar

“Stuck in traffic” lands badly when the shared calendar says you were home.

Repeats itself

The dentist worked in March. It will not work again in April.

Sounds rehearsed

Too much detail, too neat, and a suspicious reader notices.

Round 2 · Accept

8/10

Critic: matches a calendar event, short, offers to make up the work.
